Nataraja and Nausea

Shiva dances. Feet of the wind thundering onto icy ground, steps in a circle, pirouette and turn. His eyes are closed and his neck turned up. He sees not whether he has run out of stage. He is All. No Other exists.

To feel his heedless abandon and his freedom for a moment, I attempt an approximation of Shiva. Instead I find inside a subtle resentment of the Other. My stage is cut up and bounded at every turn. Shiva has it easier than me.

To be sure, it is not the external resistance which carves out my world that bothers me. I accept the dues I must pay for being in a world of beings. Once the canals are dug in however, I simply wish to flow through them freely. The stage I speak of is internal. I accept the Other as such but wish to be free of the part of me that is routinely plagued by its unreadable gaze.

At the notion of being twisted and tossed inside my own mind at the whim of the Other, I am overcome with disgust. Who is bigger than me in my own mind? Who are any of you to submit me into movement or binding in my own world? Disgust, it is disgust that coats every morsel of food down my throat. I cannot bear the shame of being shackled in my backyard.

Disgust forces my hand the way no fact or sentence could. It is instinctive, just as a child vomits out food he cannot bear. I wipe the vomit from my face and stand up off the ground. My chest feels lighter and my breath returns to me. Disgusted at being moved by another's will, like a courtesan and a puppet, my mind goes quiet for one moment. The gaze disappears for one moment.
Does a courtesan find pockets of infinity during her evening performance before the king? Patches of time she enters, with closed eyes, where she turns on her heels as if alone in the world? Where the grand embellished walls of the castle fall away and she's laid bare to the night sky?
Then the patch goes away perhaps, and the king's gaze re-appears. She sees the lust in his eyes tinged with the intoxication of the power he has over her. She cannot shake these feelings even when she dances under a tree alone. At this moment in court, she gags with her back to him. The next pocket of infinity will come soon, and she will be free for some time again.
