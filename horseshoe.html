Horseshoe

One wants to Live. Much. Stay alive above all. Keep safe and avoid suspicious people. Avoid dark corners and street dealers. Avoid Fire and be wary of Fanged Animals. Keep safe brother a heat wave rages outside. Turn the lights off! See that predator with his nose pressed up against the window baring his teeth? Grab the gun and keep it on you. Do not make a sound. Shit. Do not move. Do not breathe. Could the predator lock-pick? Could he shoot first? Could he bludgeon my head into the floor? If I shoot first he will not get me. If I shoot myself first the heat wave will not find me. No edge of a blade will find me if I do myself in first. I will be kept safe from it all in the soft arms of death. Death will keep me alive best. Nothing will keep me alive from Everything.

—

Abel, Babel, Caleb. Young Men.
