Horseshoe

You want to Live. Much. Don't you? You want to stay alive above all? Good. To be sure of staying alive, you must keep safe and avoid suspicious people. Avoid dark corners and street dealers. Avoid Fire and be wary of Fanged Animals. Keep safe brother a heat wave rages outside. Turn the lights off! See that predator with his nose pressed up against the window baring his teeth? Grab the gun and keep it on you. Do not make a sound. Shit. Do not move. Do not breathe. Could the predator lock-pick? Could he shoot first? Could he bludgeon my head into the floor? Quick, come into the cabinet with me and lock it from the inside. You can't breathe? So? Do you wanna be killed? Shut up and Stop moving. We will be safe here. Unmoving and unbreathing, away from every edge and every hand and every movement in the wild. So soft, this gentle asphyxiation. This atrophy of all muscle, and all potential danger from this lack of movement. How soft and gentle and placating. How soft this death is, how safe. See how much safer we are here, away from everything? I will keep you safe in here brother. We will be kept safe from it all in these soft arms of death. Nothing moves here, in this soft death. Nothing will get you here. Death will keep us alive best. Nothing will keep us alive from Everything.

—
